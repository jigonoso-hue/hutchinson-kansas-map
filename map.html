            Road rules. Walkers can cut across open ground and parks but not through built-up blocks, water or highways (roads and bridges are always open). Runners and cyclists stay on roads, and nobody on foot uses the highways. Use Mixed to drive part of the way, park, and finish on foot. Cars go 25 mph on streets, 35 on main roads and 55 on highways, slowed by traffic that follows the time of day. If parking and walking beats driving all the way, Drive and Mixed say so. Every 15 minutes of running at a stretch costs 1 level of exhaustion, so 30 minutes is 2 levels. Levels last until 1 hour after you stop. Jocks ignore it.

            What time is it in Hutchinson?

            Traffic follows the clock. School drop-off, lunch, evening rush and Friday night football all slow the roads.
