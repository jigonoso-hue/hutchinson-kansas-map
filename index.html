Career Day is the day before Homecoming

St. Louis
Friday, April 8

Senior trip to St. Louis

In the spring, the Class of 2005 will board the buses for St. Louis. The Gateway Arch will be waiting on the riverbank, a curve of silver steel that looks less like a monument than a door. Chaperones will ask everyone to stay with their group and count heads twice. The seniors will ride to the top, look west over the river, and wonder what the road ahead holds. Some doors only open once. Pack warm, and plan to walk through it together.
Theatre Dept. · Winter
January 28 & 29

Winter show: Brigadoon

By Staff Reporter. This winter the theatre department will stage Brigadoon, the story of a Scottish village that is on no map and wakes for just one day every hundred years. Two lost travelers will stumble into it, and one of them will have to decide whether love is worth the world he left behind. Expect a story about hidden places, belonging, and what you owe the people who stay.
Theatre Dept. · Spring
April 22 & 23

Spring show: Cats

By Staff Reporter. This spring the theatre department will stage Cats. On one night a year, the Jellicle cats will gather in a junkyard under the moon, and the old cat who leads them will choose one to leave the rest and be reborn. Every cat will get a song and a reason to be picked. The cast will prowl through the aisles, and the lights will stay low. Expect a story about being seen and what it costs the ones left behind. The night will end with one cat chosen, and the chance to ascend.

Find your crowd

Every hallway at Hutchinson High belongs to somebody. Choose one crowd, spend six standing points, and make it count. Every +1 standing with a crowd is a -1 with its enemy. Your crowd also sets your starting loadout, shown when you pick a crowd, with 5e-style rules (DCs are suggestions for the DM). Everyone also carries a backpack, a student ID and a class schedule. Cell phones are rare in 2004, and only the Popular Kids start with one. Meet the leaders of each crowd (PDF).
